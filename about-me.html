About me

Zhiqi Li is an interdisciplinary artist and artistic researcher who holds a Bachelor of Fine Arts and a Master of Fine Arts in Oil Painting from Hubei Institute of Fine Arts, as well as a Master of Arts in Information Experience Design from the Royal College of Art, London. Originating from an exploration of perception and representation in visual art, her artistic practice has gradually expanded into sound art, interactive technologies, and ecological media research. Her work investigates how sound can reveal invisible environmental processes and reconsider the perceptual relationships between humans, technology, and the non-human world.

Her practice integrates field recording, environmental data sonification, embodied sensing, generative sound, and spatial sound installations, transforming issues such as air pollution, urban ecological transformation, and cross-species interaction into auditory and embodied experiences. Her key projects include Poetry of City, Sensing the Unseen, and Beako, a research project exploring sound-mediated interspecies interaction. Through her ongoing artistic research, she seeks to move beyond the conventional understanding of sound as a medium for communicating information, exploring its potential as a generative and relational medium that fosters embodied perception, ecological awareness, and new understandings of human–non-human coexistence.
