About me

Zhiqi Li is an interdisciplinary artist and artistic researcher with a background in Fine Arts and Information Experience Design from the Hubei Institute of Fine Arts and the Royal College of Art. Her practice spans sound art, interactive technologies, and ecological media, exploring the relationships between human perception, environmental processes, and the non-human world.

Integrating field recording, environmental data sonification, embodied sensing, and generative sound, her work investigates how invisible ecological phenomena can be translated into auditory and embodied experiences. Through practice-based research, she explores sound as a generative and relational medium for fostering ecological awareness and rethinking human–non-human relationships.
