The Butterfly in the Brain

2023

Animation | installation | Sensing interaction

2m*3m*1.5m

In ancient China, there is Zhuanzi's butterfly dream, and in modern science there is the brain in a jar. The connection between the two makes me think that people can look back to reality in dreams. By using different sensors and frame-by-frame animation, I let the device interact with the audience and construct the connection between the real world and the virtual world for the audience, hoping to relieve pressure and regulate emotions in this way.
