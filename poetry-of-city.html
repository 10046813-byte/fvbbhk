Poetry of City

2023

Acrylic | Lime stone | Fishing line | screw

180cm*10cm*3

Deindustrialization or industrial hollowing is a social and economic process of industrialization. With the development of heavy industry, all kinds of pollution are everywhere, and the deindustrialization of cities is imminent. The poem of the city I played was intended to appeal to the need for cities to be deindustrialized, for humans and nature to live in harmony and leave a beautiful sound together.
