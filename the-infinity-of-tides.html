The Infinity of Tides

2023

Installation | Gesture interaction | Performance art

2m*2m*2m

Tides are closely related to our daily lives, but they are not well understood and studied. For ordinary people, the tide is a beautiful and spectacular natural phenomenon, for experts and scholars, the tide is a powerful physical phenomenon. Tides are a topic worthy of discussion everywhere and at any time, so I want to use the manifestations of devices and interactions to explain the ever-changing tides.
